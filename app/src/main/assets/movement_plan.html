Seated core

Done in a chair, so you never fight the floor. 2–3 days a week, 1–2 sets. Exhale on effort — never hold your breath. Green-bordered work is a hold; the rest is reps.

Low-impact resistance

One foot always grounded, no jumping. Body weight, bands, or light dumbbells. Stop 2–3 reps short of failure. Progress reps (8 to 12), then sets (1 to 2 to 3), then resistance.

Progression Timeline

Change one variable at a time, hold each step ~2 weeks.

Weeks 1–2Start here
10–15 min × 2/day, or 15–20 min × 1 · 1.8–2.2 mph · 0% incline
Goal: tolerance and habit. Split sessions count the same as continuous.
Weeks 3–4
20–25 min continuous · 2.0–2.5 mph · 0–1% incline
Goal: one unbroken bout. Keep RPE ≤ 13.
Weeks 5–8
30 min continuous, 5 days/week · 2.5–3.0 mph · 1–2% incline
Goal: hit 150 min/week. Add 2 resistance days.
Weeks 9+
30–45 min · +0.2 mph only if RPE still ≤ 13 · +1% incline every 2 weeks, cap 4–6%
Goal: build toward 200–300 min/week. Still never speed + incline in the same week.
Rule
Never increase speed and incline in the same week. Build toward 200–300 min/week for weight-loss maintenance.
Get medical clearance first
Blood pressure, blood sugar, sleep apnea, and joint status all need checking. This is general information, not a personalized prescription.

Stop and seek care for

Chest pain or pressure
Dizziness or fainting
Breathlessness out of proportion to effort
Joint pain that worsens during or after the session

Everyday rules

Handrails · For balance only — leaning cuts the work substantially
Footwear · Supportive, cushioned shoes; cushioned treadmill deck
Breathing · Exhale on effort; never hold your breath (BP spike)
Skip for now · Sit-ups, crunches, straight-leg raises, 12-3-30, jumping
Range · Pain-free range only; reduce or swap, don’t push through
Hydration · Drink before and during — dehydration worsens constipation
Diet · ~200 kcal/30 min walking — diet drives the scale, exercise drives fitness
