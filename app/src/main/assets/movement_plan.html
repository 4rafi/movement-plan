Safety at BMI 42

🩺 Get medical clearance first. Blood pressure, blood sugar, sleep apnea, and joint status all need checking. This page is general information, not a personalized prescription.

Stop and seek care for:

Chest pain or pressure
Dizziness or fainting
Breathlessness out of proportion to effort
Joint pain that worsens during or after the session

Everyday rules

🖐️ HandrailsFor balance only — leaning cuts the work substantially
👟 FootwearSupportive, cushioned shoes; cushioned treadmill deck
😮‍💨 BreathingExhale on effort; never hold your breath (BP spike)
🚫 Skip for nowSit-ups, crunches, straight-leg raises, 12-3-30, jumping
📏 RangePain-free range only; reduce or swap, don't push through
💧 HydrationDrink before and during — dehydration worsens constipation
🍽️ DietWalking burns ~200 kcal/30 min — diet drives the scale, exercise drives fitness
